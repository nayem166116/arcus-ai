About Arcus

We think operating teams deserve automation they can trust

Arcus Labs, Inc. builds AI agents that handle real operational work — with the transparency and control that operating teams need to actually rely on them.

Our story

Built by people who ran support and ops teams

We started Arcus after years of watching automation tools promise a lot and deliver brittle, opaque scripts nobody trusted. Operating teams need agents that show their reasoning, ask for help when they're unsure, and get better over time — not black boxes that quietly fail. Arcus was built to be the workspace where that kind of automation actually happens.
