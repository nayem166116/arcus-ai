The challenge

Northbeam's support team was drowning in repetitive tickets — password resets, billing questions, integration troubleshooting — while more complex issues waited in queue. Response times were slipping and the team couldn't hire fast enough to keep up.

The solution

Northbeam rolled out Arcus agents connected to Zendesk, Salesforce, and Slack. Agents triage and resolve routine tickets automatically, with approval gates for refunds and account changes. Complex cases are escalated to human agents with full context already gathered.
