Privacy Policy

Last updated 12 June 2026

1. Overview

Arcus Labs, Inc. ("Arcus", "we", "us") provides an AI agent workspace for operating teams. This policy describes what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have.

2. Information we collect

We collect account information you provide (name, email, company), workspace content you connect to Arcus (such as tickets, CRM records, and messages processed by your agents), and usage data such as run logs and feature interactions.

3. How we use information

We use this information to operate and improve Arcus, provide customer support, secure your workspace, and communicate important product updates.

4. Data sharing

We do not sell your data. We share data only with subprocessors necessary to run the service (such as cloud infrastructure providers), and only under contractual confidentiality obligations.

5. Data retention

We retain workspace data for as long as your account is active, and for a limited period afterward as required for legal or operational reasons.

6. Your choices

You can access, export, or delete your workspace data at any time from your account settings, or by contacting us directly.

7. Contact us

Questions about this policy can be sent to hello@arcus.com.
